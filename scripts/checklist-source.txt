# World Cup 2026 Sticker Checklist: 980-Code Collector Reference

Use this fan-maintained page as a collector reference for the Panini FIFA World Cup 2026 album: opening foils, FIFA Museum entries, team badges, team photos, and player stickers. We update names from public pack evidence, official product pages, and reader corrections rather than claiming a public machine-readable official checklist. Search by code, team, player, category, or rarity, then mark the cards already in your album. When you want analytics, open the tracker; when you want to trade, move to the missing list.

Open the missing list View the tracker

## What is inside the Panini WM 2026 album?

The album splits cleanly into three parts. The opening section contains 9 foil stickers covering the Panini logo, official emblems, mascots, slogan, ball, and three host country panels. The FIFA Museum section follows with 11 chronological foils for selected past World Cup winners. The remaining 960 stickers cover all 48 qualified teams at 20 stickers per team (one team-badge foil pair, one team photo, and 18 player stickers).

### 980 stickers total

The album size reported by Panini at launch. This page tracks the collector checklist we can verify and marks corrections when stronger source evidence appears.

### 48 national teams

The first World Cup with 48 participating nations. Browse each team in the Teams section for a focused profile.

### 68 special stickers

Special stickers include foil and insert-style entries that collectors usually chase first. Read more in our World Cup history page.

## How to use the checklist efficiently

Most opening sessions take less than 10 minutes if you use the right input mode. Pick the workflow that fits your pace:

### Search and filter

Type a sticker code, player name, or team name in the search bar to jump to a specific entry. Combine with the Group, Team, Category, and Rarity filters to narrow the grid to exactly the slice you care about.

### Bulk entry for full boxes

After opening a 50-pack box, paste every code into the bulk entry box, separated by spaces or commas, and click Mark Owned. The tool processes every code in one click and updates the missing list immediately.

### Quick code chips

Open the Quick Code Selector to see every visible sticker code as a clickable chip. Tap each code to toggle the owned state. This view is fastest on mobile when you know the codes but not the names.

### Duplicate counter

Increment the duplicate counter every time you pull an extra of the same sticker. The missing list reads this value to build a clean duplicate export you can paste into a trade thread.

## Checklist FAQ

Common questions specifically about the checklist tool. General questions about the project are answered on the homepage.

How is the 980-sticker checklist structured?

The checklist follows the same order as the official Panini album. The first 9 entries are opening stickers (logo, two emblems, mascots, slogan, ball, and three host country foils). Stickers FWC9 to FWC19 are the FIFA Museum foils for selected past World Cup champions. The remaining 960 entries are the 48 team sections of 20 stickers each.

What filters does the checklist support?

You can filter by World Cup group (A through L), by team, by sticker category (Brand / Emblem, Host / Tournament, FIFA Museum, Team Badge, Team Photo, Player), by rarity (Base or Foil), and by owned status. Search works on sticker code, player name, and team name simultaneously.

What is the fastest way to mark a full box opening?

Use the bulk entry box at the top of the checklist tool. Paste every code you pulled, separated by spaces or commas, and click Mark Owned. The tool processes every code in one click and updates both the tracker dashboard and the missing list immediately.

How do I track duplicate stickers in the checklist?

Each sticker card has a duplicate counter with plus and minus buttons. Every time you pull an extra copy, increment the counter. The duplicate value is used by the missing list tool to generate trade-ready exports.
